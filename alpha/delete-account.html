Delete your account

This removes your account from this game server: your connected company, its synced actions, raids, sessions, recovery codes, alliance membership, support tickets and privacy settings. It cannot be undone.

Messages and posts you wrote stay visible as "deleted" so conversations still make sense; moderation records are kept for up to a year, then removed. Local companies on your own devices are not stored on the server and are not affected. You can also delete the account in the game under Online → Account.
