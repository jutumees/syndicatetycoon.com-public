Delete your account

Your account is suspended straight away and every device is signed out. After 30 days it is removed from this game server for good: your connected company, its synced actions, raids, sessions, recovery codes, your email address, linked sign-in accounts, alliance membership, support tickets and privacy settings.

Changed your mind? Sign in to the game again within those 30 days and the deletion is cancelled. After that it cannot be undone.

Messages and posts you wrote stay visible as "deleted" so conversations still make sense; moderation records are kept for up to a year, then removed. Local companies on your own devices are not stored on the server and are not affected. You can also delete the account in the game under Online → Account.
